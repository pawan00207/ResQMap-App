Archive Protocol FAQ

How do I register as a primary responder? expand_more
Navigate to the Dashboard and click 'Apply for Responder Status'. You will need to provide valid emergency certification and complete a short verification protocol.
Are my location details encrypted? expand_more
Yes, ResQMap utilizes AES-256 bit encryption for all personal telemetry. Your precise location is only visible to active emergency responders during an SOS event.
How often is the resource map updated? expand_more
Data synchronization occurs every 60 seconds. Critical updates like shelter availability or flood warnings are pushed instantly via the live notification panel.
Can I use ResQMap offline? expand_more
The Sentinel Archive allows you to download regional data packets. In offline mode, you can access previously cached maps and emergency contacts.
What are the regional tier classifications? expand_more
Zones are classified from Tier 1 (Stable) to Tier 4 (Critical Crisis). These tiers dictate the level of resource automation and external aid mobilization.
Who can I contact for technical support? expand_more
Technical support is available 24/7 through the 'Contact' portal or by emailing support@resqmap.org. Priority response is given to active emergency zones.
